The boundary

Snowboard Math keeps labeled-norm arithmetic only. Weight charts, mondo conversion, outsole and stance figures are labeled norms, not measurements of your body, your boots or your boards - brands size differently, boots pack out, and style preference is real. Try the board or trust the shop's fit session over anything this page says.

Stand a board on its tail and every chin gets the same answer, whatever the rider weighs. The sizing chart starts from kilograms instead. And length is only the start: a board can be the right length and still drag a size-twelve toe, and its reference stance can sit inches from the one your height suggests. Snowboard Math does the honest arithmetic between the rider, the boots and the board.

Three panels. The length truth reads the weight chart, adjusted for style. The width truth weighs the boot's sole against the waist's drag line. The stance truth weighs the board's reference stance against the rider's height.
